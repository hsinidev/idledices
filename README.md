Idledices: A Comprehensive Guide



In "Idledices", you take on the role of a time-traveling agent tasked with repairing anomalies in the space-time continuum.



Game Overview


Idledices is a puzzle-platformer game that challenges players to navigate through increasingly complex levels, fixing temporal distortions and restoring balance to the timestream.



How to Play


To play, use your mouse or keyboard keys to control your agent, interacting with objects, platforms, and enemies. Your goal is to collect resources, repair anomalies, and progress through levels.

Tips and Tricks for Success
